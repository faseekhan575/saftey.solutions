SS Safety Solutions - Premium Safety Equipment & Protective Gear in Pakistan

Welcome to SS Safety Solutions, Pakistan's trusted partner in premium safety equipment, personal protective gear (PPE), fire fighting systems, rescue equipment, road safety solutions, and certified industrial safety tools. Established in 2010, SS Safety Solutions provides certified and high-performance safety products across Pakistan to protect lives, secure industrial workplaces, and empower emergency responders.

Our Comprehensive Product Categories

  • Security & Tactical Gear: Ballistic helmets, tactical vests, body armor, and protective suits for high-risk operations.
  • Rescue Equipment: Emergency stretchers, rescue ropes, fall arrest harnesses, and specialized rescue tools.
  • Fire Fighting Equipment & Vehicles: Fire extinguishers, fire hoses, nozzles, fire alarms, and fire emergency vehicles.
  • Safety Shoes & Protective Footwear: Steel-toe boots, anti-slip work shoes meeting EN ISO 20345 standards.
  • Road Safety & Traffic Management: Traffic cones, reflective signs, speed bumps, and road barriers.
  • Personal Protective Wear (PPE): High-visibility jackets, flame-resistant coveralls, safety goggles, and work gloves.
  • Medical & Ambulance Equipment: First aid kits, defibrillators, and emergency medical equipment.
  • Laboratory Safety & Containment: Spill prevention kits, hazardous chemical storage, and lab safety goggles.
  • Industrial & Power Tools: Heavy-duty hand tools, power drills, and industrial maintenance tools.
